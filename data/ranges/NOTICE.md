Opponent range data: Cash_100_GTO.json

Source: jensbaagaard/poker-practice, data/openSourcePokerData/Cash_100_GTO.json
https://github.com/jensbaagaard/poker-practice/tree/main/data/openSourcePokerData
The source repository describes itself as MIT licensed and says its range data
is free to use. The set contains 100bb, 6-max cash preflop GTO frequencies
using the default 2.5bb open size. Frequencies are preserved as supplied.

This is a theoretical preflop baseline, not a read on a specific opponent.
